Frequently Asked Questions

Q. How do I enter notes?

Tap the spot on the staff where you want a note. A note at that pitch is added and played right away. Choose a note length (whole note to sixteenth note) in the bottom toolbar first.

Q. How do I make a chord?

Turn on the Chord button in the bottom toolbar, then add notes. They stack on the selected note and sound together.

Q. I can't hear any sound.

  • Check your device's Silent mode (side switch) and media volume.
  • If only the metronome is silent, check whether the metronome is muted in the playback settings.

Q. Why does the app need the camera?

Only to photograph paper sheet music and show it on screen as a reference image you can copy from. Photos you take never leave your device. Even without camera access, you can load images from your photo library.

Q. Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 16.0 or later. The app is optimized for landscape orientation.
